Before you book

FAQ

Draft answers — the studio will confirm them before launch.

How do I book a session?

Message us on WhatsApp using any "Book on WhatsApp" button. Tell us your idea, where you want it and roughly how big, and we will reply with availability.

Can I bring my own design or reference?

Yes. Send reference images in the WhatsApp chat and the artist will talk you through how to adapt them into a custom piece.

How do I choose an artist?

Browse our artists and the gallery above. Each artist has their own style, so pick the one whose work is closest to what you have in mind, or ask us and we will point you to the best fit.

What are your studio hours?

The opening hours and a map are in the Visit the studio section above.
